\section{Overview}

This document is the internal research report for a five-iteration investigation into whether early structural signals in a scientific concept's topic co-occurrence network anticipate its later cross-field breadth. The study used the OpenAlex bulk snapshot (476,196,327 works; 2026-09-23) and screened 53 early network indicators across six families against a five-feature popularity baseline on held-out field groups.

The run commissioned 25 artifacts across five iterations; 21 completed and 4 failed or were left incomplete (Table~\ref{tab:artifacts}). Each iteration below records what was planned, what was built, every result table produced, the reviewer's verdict, and the hypothesis update that moved the run forward. Dead ends are kept and labelled.

\subsection{Outcome: rarefied field breadth}

The primary outcome is rarefied field breadth $O_{2r}$ ($m = 50$): the expected number of distinct venue fields among a fixed-size random draw of $m$ papers from a concept's publications in years $t_0 + 6$ to $t_0 + 8$, computed by exact hypergeometric rarefaction.

\section{Iteration 1: Wide screen of rival spread signals}
\label{sec:iter1}

\subsection{Strategy and rationale}

Iteration~1 ran a wide screen across five rival mechanisms for why a new concept becomes broadly integrated. The five mechanisms were: (1)~lineage naturalisation (background-adjusted~$A^*_h$), (2)~social reach through unconnected author groups, (3)~structural diversity of new co-occurrence neighbours, (4)~frequency-free selectivity, and (5)~landing in gateway fields plus adopter insularity. All were screened on one frozen dev panel (48 concepts, CS/Eng/BGM/Med homes, onset 2003--2009), with one baseline, one outcome set and one pre-registered rule.

The shared OpenAlex credit pool ran dry during this iteration (139 own credits spent by Experiment~1), forcing data deviations: yearly counts used OpenAlex S0 exactly, but field labels and citation lineage came from free Semantic Scholar data.

Five artifacts were commissioned; three completed, and two failed.

Two artifacts failed due to REPL timeout:

\subsection{Review and hypothesis update}

The reviewer scored iteration~1 at 3 (blocking). Key critiques: several conclusions contradicted artifact evidence (B5 baseline $\rho$ was only 0.327 for Experiment~4, not 0.77--0.83 as claimed; the $A^*_h$ medians were actually within-group correlations; ``strongest secondary signal'' was false). Two failed artifacts were omitted entirely.

The hypothesis update moved to \textbf{deepen}: the best strand was a lead (gateway retention $\Delta$AUC $+0.10$, dev only, $n=80$). The unit of analysis shifted to the concept$\times$field episode (justified by $\tau_{cj} = 0.65 > \tau_c = 0.29$). $A^*_h$ and $D_{\text{ratio}}$ were closed as headline bets. Confidence decreased.

\section{Iteration 2: Testing gateway retention at scale}
\label{sec:iter2}

\subsection{Strategy and rationale}

Iteration~2 moved the budget from more metrics to more samples, as the reviewer demanded. One zero-credit scan of all 2,040 OpenAlex S3 works files produced a 12,499-concept panel with 27,393 episodes and a hash-sealed held-out split. Five artifacts were commissioned, all completed.

\subsection{Experiment 5: Sealed held-out test of gateway retention}
% Artifact workspace: gen_art_experiment_5

Built the authoritative S1 frame tables. The hash-sealed spec was unsealed once.

\textbf{H1 (gateway retention) --- DISCONFIRMED.} Held-out $\Delta$AUC $= -0.00001$ [$-0.0006$, $+0.0003$]; DL pooled $-0.00004$ ($I^2 = 0$). The baseline ladder showed gateway's DEV signal ($+0.0019$) vanished once the leave-concept-out field propensity $P_j(-c)$ was added, and reversed on held-out data. The shuffled-$R$ placebo's 95th percentile (0.130) exceeded the original $+0.103$, so the iteration-1 lead could not be certified as above chance on 80 episodes. Minimum detectable $\Delta$AUC was 0.004.

\textbf{H3 (concept-level gateway landing):} Held-out partial $\rho$: $G=0.030$, $G_A=0.026$, $G_{\text{btw}}=0.046$ (Holm $p=0.0045$); DL pooled $G = 0.068$ [$0.029$, $0.107$]. However, the concept-bootstrap CI of pooled $G$ included zero ([$-0.006$, $+0.065$]), and DEV-to-held-out shrinkage was from 0.14 to 0.03.

\subsection{Experiment 6: Where new scientific concepts spread next}
% Artifact workspace: gen_art_experiment_6

Conditional logit on 653 newborn concepts (1,865 episodes). Relatedness to retained fields predicted the next field entered: held-out $d_0 = 0.30$ [$0.24$, $0.37$], LR 71.7, permutation $p = 0.001$, rewired $p = 0.015$. Gateway weighting added nothing beyond plain retaining relatedness (M3 vs M1: $p = 0.17$).

DTW trajectory classes ($k=2$) were stable (bootstrap ARI 1.0), but HMM gave ARI $= 0.094$ against the DTW classes, so the two-class typology was not reproduced.

\subsection{Evaluation 1: Stress-testing the gateway retention lead}
% Artifact workspace: gen_art_evaluation_1

Verdict: \textbf{FAILS.} The union-panel $\Delta$AUC was $+0.001$ [$-0.012$, $+0.012$]. The D1 all-$G$-variant O1 gains ($+0.05$--$+0.15$) were label-coverage artefacts ($G$: $+0.072 \to +0.002$ after controlling for coverage). The field intercept $R^2$ was 0.03 ($p = 0.55$) on the union panel.

\subsection{Research 1: Positioning study}
% Artifact workspace: gen_art_research_1

Reviewer score: 3 (blocking). Gateway retention was honestly disconfirmed, but several conclusions still contradicted evidence (``disconfirmed by all preregistered criteria'' contradicted the positive within-field LPM; ordering was listed as ``confirmed'' despite negative lead-lag coefficients).

Hypothesis moved to \textbf{deepen}: headline shifted from gateway centrality (closed) to the retained-frontier lead from Experiment~6 ($d_0 = +0.281$). The RQ1 held-out indicator screen was made mandatory. Confidence decreased.

\section{What the run learned}
\label{sec:learned}

\subsection{Confirmed findings}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Seven early indicators predict cross-field breadth beyond B5} (Experiment~8, iteration~3). The confirmed indicators span two families: retained frontier (M0\_density\_end, D\_vol\_end, CONTACT\_REACH, RETENTION\_RATIO) and co-occurrence ego-network topology ($n_{\text{comm}}$, NOV$_{\text{res}}$, ego\_density). About half of the top two is pre-onset footprint.

\item \textbf{The OPEN index captures early neighbourhood openness} (Experiment~10, iteration~4; Evaluation~4, iteration~5). The Eval4 descriptive pool of OPEN$_{\text{home}}$ over 6 non-selection bodies gives partial Spearman $+0.069$ [$+0.038$, $+0.100$], $I^2 = 0$, 6/6 positive. The home-only signal is marginal (DL CI includes zero on the fresh cohort), with no practical forecasting gain ($+0.002$).

\item \textbf{Concepts spread to fields related to those retaining them} (Experiment~7, iteration~3). $d_0 = 0.322$ [$0.291$, $0.355$] on the independent frame, extending the principle of relatedness. However, the volume-matched contrast is null, and the effect reverses under min-cp proximity.

\item \textbf{Consistency predicts volume but narrower breadth} (Experiment~14, iteration~5). Cheng et~al.'s consistency $+53.5\%$/SD for volume drops to $+1.3\%$ once size is controlled. As an early trait, consistency predicts \emph{less} breadth: $-0.069$ [$-0.093$, $-0.047$], $I^2 = 0$, 5/5 negative.

\item \textbf{Breadth is 73\% early exploration, 27\% retention} (Experiment~12, iteration~4). Broad concepts start with wider contact, not by advancing a wider frontier ($s_M = -0.046$).

\item \textbf{The signal is topical non-redundancy, not temporal turnover} (Experiment~16, iteration~5). A within-concept permutation null absorbs the year-to-year component while the signal persists.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Dead ends}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Citation naturalisation gap $A^*_h$}: null ($\Delta\rho = -0.006$, iteration~1). Medians were negative in all groups.
\item \textbf{Gateway field retention}: disconfirmed ($\Delta$AUC $= -0.00001$, iteration~2). Gateway was a domain-specific proxy for ``fields that keep things.''
\item \textbf{Social reach (candidate S)}: tested in iteration~3, not confirmed for any outcome.
\item \textbf{Gateway weighting for field entry}: adds nothing beyond plain retaining relatedness (iteration~2).
\item \textbf{Discrete trajectory typology}: failed naming rule (DTW vs HMM ARI $= 0.222$, iteration~4). Breadth is a continuum.
\item \textbf{Within-concept closure $\to$ entry slowdown}: null on DEV ($b = -0.070$, $+0.015$; iteration~4), opposite sign on held-out (iteration~5).
\item \textbf{Ordering (gateway first $\to$ take-off)}: mixed. Negative lead-lag, pre-trend, reverse path significant on DEV.
\item \textbf{External recognition (O5)}: unrelated to publication outcomes ($\rho = 0.014$, iteration~3).
\item \textbf{Abandonment penalty}: inconclusive (specification-dependent; iterations~3--5).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{What is still open}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Causal mechanism}: the run shows association, not causation. Early openness could reflect intrinsic generality, community diversity, or problem breadth.
\item \textbf{Domain heterogeneity}: Life \& Environment shows the weakest OPEN signal (PSP $+0.071$ vs $+0.186$ for others pooled); the retained-field relatedness effect has $I^2 = 0.92$.
\item \textbf{Temporal turnover vs static dispersion}: V2 excess has split-half reliability below 0.05, so the question cannot be adjudicated at typical sample sizes. Temporal turnover is untested rather than refuted.
\item \textbf{Vocabulary survivorship}: Frame~N was small (636 concepts) with pre-unseal power of 0.47.
\item \textbf{Measurement noise}: split-half reliability of OPEN$_{\text{home}}$ is 0.49. Individual-concept prediction is imprecise.
\end{enumerate}
