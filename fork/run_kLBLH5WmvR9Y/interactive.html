The finding

Which early signals in a concept's topic network tell us whether it will later spread across many fields?

Popularity already explains a lot. A five-feature reaches Spearman 0.65–0.86 with later breadth, so any network signal has to add something beyond it. The paper screens 53 early indicators and composes the survivors into the : a concept's early tendency to acquire novel, community-spanning co-occurrence partners.

Measured on the concept's home field only, and net of the baseline, OPEN has the same sign in every body of data that was not used to choose it:

The association is small, and the pool is descriptive: all non-selection bodies except the 2015–17 cohort had outcomes read in earlier analyses. It adds almost nothing to a forecast. What it shows is a consistent direction: concepts whose early partners span more topic communities reach more fields later.

      What the paper does not show

      • No forecasting gain. OPENhome adds +0.002 [−0.003, +0.008] Spearman over B5 on the fresh cohort.
      • Marginal on the home-only build. On the 2015–17 cohort it passes rung R2 (+0.091, 95% CI [+0.013, +0.171], Holm p = 0.048), but its DerSimonian–Laird interval over groups includes zero (+0.083 [−0.007, +0.173]).
      • Static, not temporal. A within-concept year-permutation null absorbs the churn: excess NOVCHURN gives +0.008 against +0.116 raw. The temporal-excess measure has split-half reliability of about 0.01–0.05, so temporal turnover is untested rather than refuted.
      • Not a within-concept mechanism. The closure test is null on DEV and opposite-signed on held-out fields (−0.079 [−0.146, −0.013]).
      • Coupling. About half of the OPENall signal is mechanical: early off-home papers enter both the ego network and the breadth outcome.
      • Noise and domains. Split-half reliability of OPENhome is 0.49. Life & Environment shows the weakest signal (PSP +0.042).
      • Retained frontier. The volume-matched contrast is null, the dose response is not monotone, and d0 reverses under minimum-conditional-probability proximity. Persistence and volume stay confounded, and the effect is specific to the PMI backbone.
      • Not causal, and survivor-selected. Openness could reflect a concept's generality or its community. The legacy vocabulary is survivor-selected; the vocabulary-free Frame N has 636 concepts and a partial verdict.
