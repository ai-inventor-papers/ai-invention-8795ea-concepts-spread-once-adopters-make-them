            What the paper does not show

            • No forecasting gain. OPENhome adds +0.002 [−0.003, +0.008] Spearman over B5. It characterises concepts; it does not forecast them better.
            • Not causal. Openness could reflect a concept's intrinsic generality, the diversity of the community around it, or the breadth of the problems it addresses. The within-concept closure test is null on DEV and opposite-signed on held-out fields (−0.079 [−0.146, −0.013]).
            • Temporal turnover is untested, not refuted. The temporal-excess variants have split-half reliability of about 0.01–0.05, so they cannot adjudicate churn at roughly 10 home papers a year. Split-half reliability of OPENhome is 0.49.
            • Coupling. About half of the OPENall signal is mechanical coupling between off-home papers in the ego network and the breadth outcome. OPENhome avoids it but is marginal.
            • Retained frontier is PARTIAL. The is null, the effect reverses under instead of the , and the dose response is not monotone. Persistence and volume stay confounded. Heterogeneity is high (I2 = 0.92).
            • Vocabulary and prior unsealing. The legacy concept vocabulary is survivor-selected; the vocabulary-free frame N has 636 concepts and pre-unseal power of 0.47. Held-out outcomes had been unsealed by two earlier experiments, so the indicator screen is a robustness check.
