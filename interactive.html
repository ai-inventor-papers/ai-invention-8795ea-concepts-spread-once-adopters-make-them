The finding

When a new concept enters one more scientific field, which field does it pick?

A field related to the fields that have adopted the concept and kept publishing on it. This holds beyond relatedness to the concept's home field, field size and the usual density measures.

It is positive in all four held-out domain groups and in the 2010–2014 onset cohort. The gain in discrimination is small, though, and a volume-matched contrast is null, so the paper's own preregistered verdict is partial: persistence is confounded with volume.

      What the paper does not show

      • Retention is not separated from volume. The preregistered is null, −0.028 [−0.105, +0.046], so the frontier verdict is partial.
      • The effect depends on how relatedness is measured. Under the coefficient reverses to −0.021 (p = 0.012) and adds no AUC.
      • Discrimination barely moves. rises from 0.847 to 0.852; field size alone reaches 0.76 and density 0.59, with only 26 fields.
      • Hub fields do not keep concepts. The adopting field's adds Δ-AUC −0.00001 [−0.0006, +0.0003] for retention on held-out episodes. Verdict: disconfirmed.
      • Math & Decision Sciences is underpowered in both the indicator screen (165 concepts) and the entry model (d0 = 0.065, interval includes zero).
      • Little forecasting value. OPEN adds +0.002 Spearman over the popularity baseline on the fresh cohort; the indicators characterise rather than forecast.
      • Held-out outcomes had been read before. Two earlier experiments unsealed them, so the screen's held-out results are robustness checks; only the 2015–2017 cohort was never used in any prior step.
      • Observational. Early structure correlates with later breadth, but the within-concept panel points to a trait across concepts, not a mechanism inside one concept's trajectory.

      Where each view's data comes from

        Glossary

        Conditional logit
        A discrete choice model that estimates the probability of entry into each candidate field within a stratified choice set. Here it is fitted by Breslow partial likelihood, stratified by each combination of concept and year.
        Risk set
        At each year after onset, every off-home field that the concept has not yet entered is at risk. The risk set is indexed by concept, target field and year.
        Retained-frontier relatedness d0
        The mean backbone relatedness between the target field and the set of off-home fields currently retaining the concept. Its coefficient is expressed in log-odds per standard deviation.
        Retaining field
        An off-home field that has adopted a concept and kept publishing on it.
        Home field
        A field holding at least 40% of a concept's first 30 grounded papers.
        Onset year t0
        The first year a concept reaches 20 grounded publications.
        Field backbone
        A weighted graph over 26 fields, with edge weights given by positive pointwise mutual information (PMI) of topics appearing jointly in the same work within a preonset window (1998–2002).
        Rarefied cross-field breadth O2r
        The expected number of distinct venue fields among a fixed-size random draw of 50 papers from a concept's publications in years t0+6 to t0+8, computed by exact hypergeometric rarefaction. Rarefaction separates size-adjusted breadth from sheer volume.
        Popularity baseline (B5)
        Five features: log publication volume at t0+2, publication growth rate, log off-home volume, off-home share, and number of home fields.
        Partial Spearman correlation
        The rank correlation between an indicator and the outcome after controlling for the popularity baseline (and, at higher rungs, further controls).
        DerSimonian–Laird pool
        A random-effects average of the per-group estimates; I2 reports how much of their spread reflects genuine heterogeneity across groups.
        Holm–Bonferroni correction
        A stepdown procedure controlling the familywise error rate across the indicators tested.
        Within-stratum AUC
        AUC computed within each concept-year stratum: how well a model ranks the entered field above the fields not entered in the same choice set.
        Minimum conditional-probability proximity
        An alternative relatedness between fields, following Hidalgo et al. (2007), used as a sensitivity rebuild in place of backbone PMI.
        Volume-matched contrast
        The preregistered comparison of entry rates of retained versus fields that were entered but not retained, in the same volume cell.
        Gateway centrality
        A field's eigenvector centrality on the backbone.
        Openness index OPENhome
        The mean of six z-scored ego-network components, with constants frozen on the full frame of all 12,499 concepts, measured within the home field.
        Neighbourhood novelty
        The share of new partners among a concept's co-occurrence neighbours.
        Early contact, frontier advance, retention
        Early contact E2 counts fields entered by t0+2; frontier advance M is the ratio of fields entered by t0+8 to those entered by t0+2; retention ρ is the share of entered fields retained at t0+8. Their logs add up to the log of retained off-home breadth.
