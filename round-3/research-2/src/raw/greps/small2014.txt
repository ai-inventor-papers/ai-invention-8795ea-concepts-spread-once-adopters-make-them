## Highlights

  * •

Emerging topics across all of science are identified by combining direct citation and co-citation models of the Scopus database.

  * •

71 emerging topics from 2007 to 2010 are identified and characterized.

  * •

3 non-exclusive motive forces are identified: scientific discovery (62%), technological innovation (38%), and exogenous events (54%).

  * •

External evidence (e.g., awards) correlates well with emerging topics.

  * •

Although nearly all emerging topics contain highly cited papers, the reverse is not true – only 10% of highly cited papers are part of emerging topics.
